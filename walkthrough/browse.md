# Tree, diagram and search

- The tree on the left lists the global components of the opened file. Switch it to All files to see the whole schema set.
- Pick a component to draw its diagram. Each box is an element with its type; the small pills are sequence, choice and all. The numbers under a box are its min and max occurs.
- Click the + on a box to open its content. Types from other files of the set open the same way.
- Type in the search box, or press /, to find an element or type in every file of the set.
- Double-click a box or a row to open the schema text at that line.
